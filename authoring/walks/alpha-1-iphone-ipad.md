# The Alpha-1 iPhone and iPad walk

What was walked, what was found and what the manuals do about it. Part of the [authoring plan](../authoring-plan.md). Written 29 October 2026 for [epic 112](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/112). The first part is the iPhone ([issue 21](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/21)), written 29 October; the second is the iPad ([issue 22](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/22) and [issue 69](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/69)), written 31 October. Decisions: DEC-183 to DEC-188. The Android phone walk is [alpha-1-android-phone.md](alpha-1-android-phone.md) and the Android tablet walk is [alpha-1-android-tablet.md](alpha-1-android-tablet.md).

## The iPhone

The app is the Alpha-1 build for iOS (shown in the app as version 0.1.0 (1)), in the Oxford and slate theme and the light look, on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5, 1206 by 2622 pixels, portrait, English (United Kingdom), the default text size) with nothing else on it. The data is the Brontë dataset only. The pictures are made by the same journeys as the Android walks. This report says what a reader can see. It names no code, no issue and no build of the application repository (DEC-040, and the standing rule that this repository is public).

### 1. What was walked

- **First run.** The Workspaces screen, with the app's own samples. Two of them (not the Brontë sample) were taken off the list with **Remove**, which also walked [Remove a tree from the list](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/remove-a-workspace.dita) to the end.
- **Preferences.** Oxford and slate and Light were chosen before any picture.
- **Create a workspace.** The New workspace box, then the New person form straight away. Patrick Brontë was added with dates and places. Maria Branwell was added from the **+** sheet, by **Basic details**.
- **Add a family.** Patrick and Maria, their marriage and Charlotte as a new child, with the small windows for her birth date and place. The family page, its tree, the sheet from the marriage mark, and the family form opened again with **Edit**.
- **A source and a note.** One source on Patrick's birth, through the whole run of screens. One note on Patrick.
- **The Photos buttons.** The camera (as far as the iPhone's question), the folder (as far as the iPhone's Files screen), the chain (the list of the tree's pictures) and the fourth button (as far as the iPhone's screen for choosing a folder), each left without adding anything.
- **A copy.** **Save as…** with both choices, through the iPhone's own save screen to the end. The GEDZIP copy was saved under On My iPhone.
- **Open a family tree file.** **Add from file…** with the GEDZIP copy, through the iPhone's Files screen to the box that follows and the new card.
- **Every place in the bar and in the More list.** Search and its **Refine by** list, a person's page and its form, an event, the sources of a fact, the **+** sheet in full with **More forms**, the three empty lists, **Preferences**, **About uFTi**, and the places that need a person with no person open.

Not walked: taking a photo (the simulator has no camera), adding a picture from the iPhone, a GEDZIP copy with pictures, the forms for a census or a register entry, landscape, the dark look, and a pass with VoiceOver.

### 3. What the tooling could not do

The pictures are copies of the whole simulator screen, with the status bar set to 10:00, Wi-Fi and a full battery, and then cut off. A box or a sheet is cut to itself. The iPhone's own Files screens, save screen, camera question and keyboard are never pictured, because they are the iPhone's, not the app's (DEC-184). The cutting and scaling are done before import, because the importer's own crop and resize run on Windows only. The iPhone's own boxes (New workspace, Remove workspace, Save as and the GEDCOM version) were answered by touch on the simulator; the rest of the app was driven by its test agent.

## The iPad

The app is the same Alpha-1 build for iOS (shown in the app as version 0.1.0 (1)), in the Oxford and slate theme and the light look, on an iPad Air 11-inch (M4) simulator (iOS 26.5, 2360 by 1640 pixels, landscape, English (United Kingdom), the default text size) with nothing else on it. The data is the Brontë dataset only. The pictures are made by the same journeys as the iPhone walk. As for the iPhone, this part says what a reader can see and names no code, no issue and no build of the application repository.

### 1. What was walked

- **First run.** The Workspaces screen, with the app's own samples. Two of them (not the Brontë sample) were taken off the list with **Remove**.
- **Preferences.** Oxford and slate and Light were chosen before any picture.
- **Create a workspace.** The New workspace box, then the New person form straight away. Patrick Brontë was added with dates and places. Maria Branwell was added from the **+** sheet, by **Basic details**.
- **Add a family.** Patrick and Maria, their marriage and Charlotte as a new child, with the small windows for her birth date and place. The family page, its tree, the sheet from the marriage mark, and the family form opened again with **Edit**.
- **A source and a note.** One source on Patrick's birth, through the whole run of screens. One note on Patrick.
- **The Photos buttons.** The camera (as far as the iPad's question, answered **Don't Allow**), the folder (as far as the iPad's Files screen), the chain (the list of the tree's pictures) and the fourth button (as far as the iPad's screen for choosing a folder), each left without adding anything.
- **A copy.** **Save as…** with both choices, through the iPad's own save screen to the end. The GEDZIP copy was saved under On My iPad.
- **Open a family tree file.** **Add from file…** with the GEDZIP copy, through the iPad's Files screen to the box that follows and the new card, and **Remove** on the extra card this made.
