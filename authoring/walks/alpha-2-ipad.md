# The Alpha-2 iPad walk

What was walked on the iPad, what was found, and what the manual does about it. Part of the [authoring plan](../authoring-plan.md) and the [walk plan](walk-plan.md). Written 11 November 2026 for [issue 169](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/169) and [issue 171](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/171). Decisions: DEC-235 and DEC-237. The walk before it is [alpha-1-iphone-ipad.md](alpha-1-iphone-ipad.md).

This report is public. It names no machine, no account, no serial number and no build number of a build that is not released. It says only what a reader can see.

## 1. What was walked

- **The build.** The same Alpha-2 build as the iPhone leg, installed fresh, from a first run with its own empty data.
- **The device.** The iPad Air 11-inch (M4) simulator, iOS 26.5, 2360 by 1640 pixels, on its side, in English (United Kingdom). One check was made upright.
- **The look.** Oxford and slate, Light.
- **The data.** The Brontë sample, and the tree that the iPhone leg built, opened from its saved copy with **Add from file…** as the walkthrough's saved state for the stages it covers (section 3 of the walkthrough). The forms were opened and filled on the iPad, and cancelled where saving would have made a second copy of a record.

| Stage | Walked |
|---|---|
| 0 | First run, the list on the left, the More list, Preferences, search, a person's page with the tree beside it, the divider, the tree, the events, upright. |
| 1 | The New workspace box, Workspaces. |
| 2 | The new person form, the name question, the edit form, the date editor and a date that is not sure. |
| 3 | The family form with a partner and a child, the family page, the tree and its marriage sheet. |
| 5 | The Notes screen and the note form. |
| 6 | Where records are kept, a source with its citation and attachment step, the evidence page. |
| 7 | Research questions and notes. |
| 9 | Search and Refine by, Save as… as far as the version question, opening a saved copy, opening a file another program made, the Remove question. |

**Not walked.** Stage 4, stage 8, saving a copy to the iPad's files, removing a tree, and the help rows on fields.

## 2. What was found

| # | Where | What a reader sees | Change from the Alpha-1 pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The list on the left | **Home**, **Person**, **Family tree**, **Events** and **More**. Workspaces is in the **More** list, which has eight entries ending with **About uFTi** and **Help**. | Home is new; Workspaces moved |
| 2 | The top | The path to the screen, a search button (not on Home), **+**, and a **Help** button with its word. | The Help button is new |
| 3 | Home | As on the iPhone: the tree's name and counts, a search box, **All people**, **Open another workspace** and **Continue with**. Search results show in two columns, people on the left and events on the right, with no **All**, **People** and **Events** buttons. | New |
| 4 | A person's page | On its side, the page is in two parts: the person on the left and the **Family tree** on the right, with its steppers, **Return to focus** and **Fit to screen**. A divider between them can be dragged. The **Person** place stays lit. Upright, the tree is not shown beside the person. | New |
| 5 | Editing a person | **Edit** opens the form in the left part, in one column, and the tree stays on the right. The new person form is one column too. | Changed: the form was in two columns |
| 6 | The name question | A small box beside the form: "“Patrick Brontë” is already in this tree." with **Use Patrick Brontë (1777–1861)** and **Add a new person called Patrick Brontë**. There is no **Cancel**; tapping outside closes it and the form stays. | New on the iPad |
| 7 | Families | The family form has two columns at the top: the partners on the left, and the marriage date, place and ages on the right. A child's row runs across the page: **Existing person** (**Set…**), then boxes for a new child, which run off the right edge. The family page lights **Family tree** in the list. | Changed |
| 8 | The tree | As on the iPhone, with **+5 siblings** beside Charlotte. The sheet from a marriage mark stays open when you leave the Family tree place, and is still there when you come back, even for another person. | The sheet: a fault |
| 9 | The More places | Each has a line under its heading that says what it is for. The Notes screen: "Your own written notes about this person, kept with their record." | New |
| 10 | Workspaces | "Each workspace is one family tree file. Open several and switch between them; your changes are saved to the file as you go." Two cards to a row. | New line |
| 11 | Create workspace | The same box as on the iPhone, starting with "My family tree". | Same |
| 12 | Sources | The same steps as on the iPhone: **Save source**, then the citation step, then **Attach source document**. The **Add source** sheet lists the sources already in the tree under **Add a new source**. | Changed |
| 13 | Save as… | The same words as on the iPhone, in small boxes beside the card with no **Cancel**: tap outside to close them. | Same as Alpha-1 |
| 14 | Opening a file | The first choice of a file in the iPad's file screen closed the screen and opened nothing; choosing it again worked. A file another program made opens the same page as on the iPhone (**Make a GEDCOM 7 copy**, **Just look**, **Cancel**). | The first choice: a fault |
| 15 | Events | The event's page is in two columns: **Event or fact** on the left and **More details** on the right. | Changed |

## 3. What the tooling could not do

- The new workspace box would not paste a name with an accent, so its picture shows the name the box starts with.
- The marriage sheet's close button answered only to a tap on the screen, not to the driver.

## 4. What the manual has now

The pictures were taken again on this build, for every key the iPad manual uses, with new alternative text. The new pictures for Home, the More list, all the people, the dates, the name question, the evidence page and another program's file are kept for the pages that will use them. The pages are corrected next: the tour, Look around, Find a person, See a family tree, Change a person, Add a family and Look at the sources of a fact all change for rows 1 to 7 and 12.
