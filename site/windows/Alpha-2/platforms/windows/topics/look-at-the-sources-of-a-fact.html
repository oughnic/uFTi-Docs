Look at the sources of a fact

See which books and records are listed for a birth, a death or another fact, and add one.

Before you begin

Open the page of Emily Brontë first. Find a person shows how.

About this task

Each fact on a person's page has a small chip that counts its sources. This page adds a source to the death of Emily Brontë.

The sheet that the chip opens lists the sources that are already in the tree. The sample tree has some. If your tree has none, the list is empty until you add a source, as Add a source to a person shows.

Procedure

  1. Choose the chip beside Death.

    It starts with Sources and a number, and ends with +. An event has one too.

    A sheet named Sources opens. It lists the sources of that fact. A fact with none says No sources for this fact yet.

  2. Choose Add source at the foot of the sheet.

    A list named Add source opens. It has a box named Search existing sources…, a button named Add a new source, and the sources already in your tree.

  3. Choose the source that backs the fact.

    If it is not in the list, choose Add a new source and fill in the form. Add a source to a person shows how to add a source to a person.

    A second step opens, with the title of the source at the top.

  4. In Page or reference number, type where to look in the source.

    For the Haworth burial register, type Burials of December 1848.

  5. In How reliable it is, choose how much weight the source deserves.
    • Not stated
    • 0 · Unreliable or estimated
    • 1 · Questionable
    • 2 · Secondary evidence
    • 3 · Direct and primary evidence

    The box starts at Not stated. The step also has Date of the entry and boxes for the words of the entry. You can leave them empty. For a copy that someone typed out, 2 · Secondary evidence is a fair choice, as the picture shows.

  6. Choose Add source.

    The sheet shows the source again, now on the list.

  7. Choose Done.

    The chip on the page shows one more source.

Results

Each source on the sheet shows its title. Under it is the page or reference you gave. A source that has an author, or a quality, can show those too, such as Quality: 2 · Secondary evidence. What a line shows depends on what you have entered for that source.

Each source on the sheet has four buttons, named Copy, Citation, Edit and Remove. The sheet also has a button named Compare records that disagree.

Good practice: give each fact its own source and its own page. Ask two questions about a source. Is it the original or a copy? Was the writer there at the time?

What to do next

To add a source to a person, see Add a source to a person. Next: Add a research note.
