# Decision log

What has been decided, what is proposed, and what still needs an answer.

Part of the [authoring plan](authoring-plan.md). Status: Draft 0.4, 16 October 2026.

**How to use this log.** A decision is *Proposed* until the maintainer accepts it in a pull request, which changes its status to *Accepted* and records the date. Questions stay open until answered, and the answer becomes a decision. When a decision changes, edit its row and note why. Do not delete history.

| DEC-117 | **The manual and the good practice guide bring out that a tree file records conclusions, while research is a process that reaches them.** A GEDCOM file, and the screens of the app, show the value a researcher currently concludes. The route to it (what each source said, where they disagreed, how they were weighed, what stays open) survives only if the researcher keeps it on purpose, in citations, research notes and a conclusion that says what it rests on. The manual teaches the tasks that keep the route. The guide explains why, how to document a conclusion drawn from conflicting evidence (theme 5), and what a GEDCOM file cannot hold (theme 8). | DEC-118 | **The manual and the good practice guide explain the default quality of the evidence the app records, and the reasons for each default.** The manual says what the default is for each kind of record and fact, and how to change it.
