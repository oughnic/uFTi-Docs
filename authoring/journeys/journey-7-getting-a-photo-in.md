# Journey 7: Getting a photo in

> "My aunt gave me a box of photographs."

Part of [WP0](README.md). Status: draft for testing, 16 October 2026. The format is from the [content model, section 4](../content-model.md#4-user-journeys). Screen names and labels are provisional until WP4 confirms them against the app.

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A box of photographs came from an aunt. Some have names and dates on the back. Some have nothing. |
| **Person** | A newcomer or a steward with a tree already. A phone with a camera. Perhaps a scanner on Windows. Not sure whether to scan or to photograph. |
| **Devices** | Android phone, with the box on the table. Or Windows, with a scanner or a folder of scans. |
| **Success** | Each photo with a name on the back is on that person's page, with who is in it and when. They can find the photo from the person. |
| **Session task** | "Here is a printed picture. Get it into the sample and put it with the right person. Say what it is." The picture is a public-domain image from the dataset's media list. |

## Steps

| # | What they try, in their words | What they ask, and what would stop them | Content that answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Take a picture of the photo, and of the back." | "A photo of a photo, is that all right?" "Scan or camera?" | Task: Add a photo from the camera. Task: Add a photo from your files. Guide: keeping photos for the long term. |
| 2 | "Put it with the right person." | "There are five people in it. Whose is it?" | Task: Link a photo to a person. Task: Link one photo to several people. To confirm the app allows it. |
| 3 | "Say who is in it and when it was taken." | "Where do I write what is on the back?" | Task: Add details to a photo: a title, a date and a note. |
| 4 | "Record where the original is." | "The original is in my aunt's box. Is that a repository?" | Task: Add a repository. Glossary: repository. |
| 5 | "Find it again." | "Where did it go?" | Task: Find a photo. Screen: the media list. |
| 6 | "Keep the photos safe too." | "Will they go in my copy?" | Quick Start page 6. Reference: file types, with and without pictures. To confirm. |

## On an Android phone

- The camera asks for permission the first time. To confirm.
- Good light, a flat photo and no shadow. The task gives those three tips and no more.

## On Windows

- A scanner or a folder of scans. The file picker lets them add several at once. To confirm.
- A scan is better than a phone picture for an old photo. The task says so.

## On a tablet or an iPhone

- Tablet: the camera as the phone, with room to see the person and the photo together.
- iPhone and iPad: the camera comes through the system picker. The iOS Simulator has no camera, so that screen is described in words with no screenshot.

## What this journey decides

- **Quick Start.** Its what-next link is "Record what you have found", shared with [Journey 3](journey-3-recording-a-find.md).
- **Task finder.** "Add a photo", "put one photo with several people", "record where a document is kept".
- **Help targets.** Add a photo, the photo page, the media list.
- **Topics first.** Add a photo from the camera. Add a photo from your files. Link a photo to a person. Add details to a photo.
- **For the app team (tier 0).** Taking a photo should lead straight to "whose is this?". The media list needs a search by name.
