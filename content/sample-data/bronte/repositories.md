# Repositories

The places that hold the sources in [sources.md](sources.md). Stage 6 adds them before the sources they hold. Only repositories we actually used, or that hold an original we cite through a transcription, are listed. Addresses are not recorded here: the website is enough for the manual, and the app's repository form is shown with the name and website only.

Online transcription services (FreeBMD, FreeREG, FreeCEN, the Cornwall Online Parish Clerks database, the Cambridge alumni database, Project Gutenberg, Wikisource and the Internet Archive) are recorded in [sources.md](sources.md) as the way a source was consulted, not as repositories. The register entry for each source says which.
