# People

The nine people of the core dataset. Stage 2 adds them with their names, sex, and birth and death as far as the sources give them. Every date and place below is copied from a source listed in [sources.md](sources.md), and every claim has a row in the [evidence register](evidence-register.md). Where sources disagree, the cell says so and points to the [research thread](research-threads.md) that weighs them.

How to read a date: a plain date is given by at least one source and contradicted by none we found. "1813 or 1814" means the sources we found disagree or only bound the year. Nothing is filled in from memory.
