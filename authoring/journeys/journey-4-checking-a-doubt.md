# Journey 4: Checking a doubt

> "Two records give different ages."

Part of [WP0](README.md). Status: draft for testing, 16 October 2026. The format is from the [content model, section 4](../content-model.md#4-user-journeys). Screen names and labels are provisional until WP4 confirms them against the app.

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A marriage record gives one age. A census taken ten years earlier gives an age that does not fit. They were told a tree should be right, and now it is not. |
| **Person** | A newcomer becoming careful, or a careful researcher. They already have records. They do not want to throw either one away, and they do not want to guess. |
| **Devices** | Windows, with two documents or two browser tabs open. Less often the phone. |
| **Success** | Both records are recorded. A research note holds the question, the evidence and what they believe, with how sure they are. The date shows it is not certain. Nothing was thrown away. |
| **Session task** | "In the sample, find the research note about an age in the 1841 census. Tell me, in your own words, what the two records say and what the note concludes." If the sample has no research note yet, give two cards that disagree and ask them to record both and write a note. |

## Steps

| # | What they try, in their words | What they ask, and what would stop them | Content that answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I have two records that do not agree." | "Do I have to pick one?" "Will the tree look wrong with two?" | Guide: evidence that disagrees, with the Brontë example of the 1841 census, which rounded adults' ages down. |
| 2 | "Put both in without choosing." | "Two births? Or one birth with two sources?" | Task: Record evidence that disagrees. How the app does this is to confirm in WP4. |
| 3 | "Mark the date as not certain." | "What does *quality* mean on a date?" "Is it *about*, or *between*?" | Task: Say how sure you are about a date. Reference: date formats and the words for uncertainty. |
| 4 | "Write down which one I believe, and why." | "Where do I write my thinking?" "What if I am still not sure?" | Task: Add a research note. Guide: write down a conclusion. Checklist: three questions before you trust a record. |
| 5 | "Come back when I find a third record." | "Will I find my note again?" | Task: Find a research note. Guide: keep a research log. |

## On an Android phone

- A long note is slow to type. The task shows the shortest useful note: the question, the two records, what they think, how sure they are.
- Switching between the record and the app is a swipe away. Nothing else differs.

## On Windows

- Two windows side by side: the record in one, uFTi in the other. The task can suggest it.
- Copy and paste from a transcription into the note. The cue says to write in your own words and to say where you looked.

## On a tablet or an iPhone

- Tablet: split screen gives the same side-by-side view as Windows.
- iPhone: the same as the phone.

## What this journey decides

- **Quick Start.** Nothing on the seven pages. Page 5 plants the seed: every fact has a source. The task finder carries this journey.
- **Task finder.** "Record two records that do not agree", "keep track of a question I am trying to answer", "put in a date I am not sure of".
- **Help targets.** The date editor, the research notes list, add or edit a research note, and the screen where evidence is weighed, once WP4 names it.
- **Topics first.** Add a research note. Say how sure you are about a date. Record evidence that disagrees. These are the guide's first worked examples, so WP9 and WP7 share them.
- **For the app team (tier 0).** The date *quality* words need plain labels or hints. A person with two conflicting facts should look deliberate, not broken.
