See a family tree

Draw the parents, partners and children of one person.

Before you begin

Open a person first. Find a person shows how.

Procedure

  1. Choose Family tree in the bar along the bottom.

    A drawing opens, with the person you chose in a box with a dark edge. Their parents are above. Their partners are beside them. Their children are below. The boxes are small, so the drawing is easiest to read when you choose Fit to screen first.

  2. Choose the round mark between two partners.

    The mark shows the year of their marriage. A sheet opens from the bottom. It names the couple and lists their marriage and each child.

  3. Choose a line in the sheet.

    The marriage line opens the page of that family. A child's line shows the tree of that child. Open family record is another way to open the family page.

  4. Use the minus and plus buttons at the top to show fewer or more generations.

    The pair beside the up arrow is for parents and older people. The pair beside the down arrow is for children and younger people.

Results

Choose Return to focus to go back to the first person. Choose Fit to screen to see the whole drawing at once. A button such as +5 siblings means more brothers and sisters are not drawn.
