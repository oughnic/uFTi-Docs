Components and licences

The parts of uFTi for the iPhone and iPad that other people made, and the licence of each.

About this page

uFTi is made with parts that other people wrote. This page names the parts that are in uFTi for the iPhone and iPad. It gives the version and the licence of each. A licence says what you may do with a part.

The list is made from the build of uFTi that this manual describes.

Words we use

Copyright
The right of the person who made something to say what may be done with it.
Library
A part of a program that someone else wrote and that other programs use.
Licence
The permission that the maker of something gives you to use it, and the rules that come with it.
Licence name
The short name of a licence, such as MIT or Apache-2.0. The names come from a public list that many programs use.
