# Journey 6: Sharing with family

> "My cousin wants a copy."

Part of [WP0](README.md). Status: draft for testing, 16 October 2026. The format is from the [content model, section 4](../content-model.md#4-user-journeys). Screen names and labels are provisional until WP4 confirms them against the app.

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | At a family gathering a cousin asked for the tree. Or they want a relative who knows one branch to help. |
| **Person** | Family steward. A big, well-worked tree with photos and notes. They care about living people's privacy. They think about who will have the tree after them. |
| **Devices** | Windows, where the tree is worked on. The phone, to send it. |
| **Success** | The cousin opened the copy and saw the same tree. The details they chose to keep back are not in it. They have their own copy somewhere safe too. |
| **Session task** | "Make a copy of the sample tree and get it to me by email or a shared folder. Then tell me what is in the copy and what is not." |

## Steps

| # | What they try, in their words | What they ask, and what would stop them | Content that answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Make a copy to send." | "Will my photos go with it?" "Which kind of file?" | Task: Export a family tree file. Reference: file types, with and without pictures. To confirm which the app offers. |
| 2 | "Leave out the living people." | "Can I keep back the birthdays of people who are alive?" "Can I send only Dad's side?" | Task: Choose what goes in a copy, if the app offers it (Q27). Guide: living people. |
| 3 | "Send it." | "It is too big for email." "Which app do I share it to?" | Task (platform): Share a file from your phone. Task (platform): Save a file on Windows and attach it. |
| 4 | "Tell them how to open it." | "They do not have uFTi." | The Quick Start link to send, and [Journey 2](journey-2-bringing-a-tree-across.md). |
| 5 | "Keep my own copy safe." | "How often?" "Where?" | Quick Start page 6. Task: Back up your tree. Guide: passing your research on. |
| 6 | "Bring their changes back in." | "They added three people. How do I get them into mine?" | No topic yet. The app has no way to merge two trees that we can document. Say so plainly, and record the ask for the app team. |

## On an Android phone

- Step 3 is the share sheet: email, a messaging app or a cloud drive.
- A big file with pictures may be too big for a message. The task says to use a drive link instead.

## On Windows

- Step 3 is a Save dialog, then attach the file in email or copy it to a shared folder.
- Step 5 is Export to a folder that is backed up, or to a USB stick.

## On a tablet or an iPhone

- iPhone and iPad: the share sheet, with AirDrop as a quick route to a relative in the same room.
- Tablet: as the phone.

## What this journey decides

- **Quick Start.** Page 6, "Keep it safe", is step 5 of this journey. The what-next page points here through the task finder.
- **Task finder.** "Send my tree to someone", "keep living people private when I share", "make a copy of my tree".
- **Help targets.** Export, the export choices, the workspace menu.
- **Topics first.** Export a family tree file. Share a copy. Back up your tree.
- **For the app team (tier 0).** The export screen should say what the copy will and will not contain. Two asks surfaced: choosing what goes in a copy (Q27) and merging changes from someone else's copy. The second belongs with the reserved sync chapter (DEC-040), not with this manual.
