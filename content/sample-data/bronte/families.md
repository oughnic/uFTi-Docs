## What the sources say about the marriages

**F01.** Gaskell says Maria was married "from her uncle's house in Yorkshire, on the 29th of December, 1812", the same day her younger sister Charlotte Branwell married in Penzance (S01). The alumni database gives "Married Maria Branwell, of Penzance, Dec. 29, 1812" (S05). The DNB says they were married on 29 December by the Rev. W. Morgan, who was married at the same service by Patrick to the daughter of Mr Fennell, Maria's uncle (S06). The plaque in St Oswald's church reads "At this rail on Monday 29th Dec. 1812 Patrick Bronte Minister of Hartshead was married to Maria Branwell" (S21). Shorter's chronology prints 18 December 1812, and his footnote records Gaskell's 29th alongside a family letter about the double wedding (S07). The register entry itself was not seen. See D01.

**F02.** Gaskell says the marriage "was fixed ... to take place on the 29th of June" (1854), and describes the service at Haworth church with Miss Wooler giving the bride away (S02). The civil marriage index lists Charlotte Bronte and Arthur Bell Nicholls in the June quarter of 1854, Keighley district, volume 9a page 201 (S12). A 2024 newspaper account gives 29 June 1854 (S24). The DNB gives 19 June 1854 (S06). See D02.

## Birth order

The order above follows the baptism dates in the Thornton register as reported by the DNB (Elizabeth 26 August 1815, Charlotte 29 June 1816, Patrick Branwell 23 July 1817, Emily Jane 20 August 1818, Anne 25 March 1820) and the statement that Maria was the eldest and born at Hartshead (S06). Gaskell agrees: "Maria Bronte, the eldest of six" (S01).
