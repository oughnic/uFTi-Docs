# The Alpha-1 iPhone and iPad walk

What was walked, what was found and what the manuals do about it. Part of the [authoring plan](../authoring-plan.md). Written 29 October 2026 for [epic 112](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/112). This first part is the iPhone ([issue 21](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/21)); the iPad follows in a part of its own. Decisions: DEC-183 to DEC-185. The Android phone walk is [alpha-1-android-phone.md](alpha-1-android-phone.md) and the Android tablet walk is [alpha-1-android-tablet.md](alpha-1-android-tablet.md).

## The iPhone

The app is the Alpha-1 build for iOS (shown in the app as version 0.1.0 (1)), in the Oxford and slate theme and the light look, on an iPhone 17 Pro simulator (iOS 26.5, 1206 by 2622 pixels, portrait, English (United Kingdom), the default text size) with nothing else on it. The data is the Brontë dataset only. The pictures are made by the same journeys as the Android walks. This report says what a reader can see. It names no code, no issue and no build of the application repository (DEC-040, and the standing rule that this repository is public).

### 1. What was walked

- **First run.** The Workspaces screen, with the app's own samples. Two of them (not the Brontë sample) were taken off the list with **Remove**, which also walked [Remove a tree from the list](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/remove-a-workspace.dita) to the end.
- **Preferences.** Oxford and slate and Light were chosen before any picture.
- **Create a workspace.** The New workspace box, then the New person form straight away. Patrick Brontë was added with dates and places. Maria Branwell was added from the **+** sheet, by **Basic details**.
- **Add a family.** Patrick and Maria, their marriage and Charlotte as a new child, with the small windows for her birth date and place. The family page, its tree, the sheet from the marriage mark, and the family form opened again with **Edit**.
- **A source and a note.** One source on Patrick's birth, through the whole run of screens. One note on Patrick.
- **The Photos buttons.** The camera (as far as the iPhone's question), the folder (as far as the iPhone's Files screen), the chain (the list of the tree's pictures) and the fourth button (as far as the iPhone's screen for choosing a folder), each left without adding anything.
- **A copy.** **Save as…** with both choices, through the iPhone's own save screen to the end. The GEDZIP copy was saved under On My iPhone.
- **Open a family tree file.** **Add from file…** with the GEDZIP copy, through the iPhone's Files screen to the box that follows and the new card.
- **Every place in the bar and in the More list.** Search and its **Refine by** list, a person's page and its form, an event, the sources of a fact, the **+** sheet in full with **More forms**, the three empty lists, **Preferences**, **About uFTi**, and the places that need a person with no person open.

Not walked: taking a photo (the simulator has no camera), adding a picture from the iPhone, a GEDZIP copy with pictures, the forms for a census or a register entry, landscape, the dark look, and a pass with VoiceOver.

### 2. What was found

| # | Where | What a reader sees | What the manual does |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The bar along the bottom | Five places, as on the Android phone: **Workspaces**, **Person**, **Family tree**, **Events** and **More**. **More** lists **Notes**, **Where records are kept**, **Who made this tree**, **Research questions and notes**, **Preferences** and **About uFTi**. | The iPhone pages are the Android phone's, written again where the iPhone differs. |
| 2 | The top of every screen | A line names the tree and the page. On **Workspaces** the name of the tree shows twice. On a page further in, a back button comes first and a long name is cut off, such as "Charlotte Br…". | Pictures are not cropped for it. |
| 3 | First run | The app starts in the **Ink and parchment** theme, following the system's light or dark setting. | The pictures are taken after choosing Oxford and slate and Light. |
| 4 | Saving the first person | The person's page opens, part of the way down, with a short "Saved" message. Saving a later person goes back to the screen it came from. | [Add your first person](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/add-your-first-person.dita) follows it. |
| 5 | **+** | The **Add a record** sheet fills the screen. **More forms** shows a count, such as "More forms (9)". | [Add another person](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/add-another-person.dita) says so. |
| 6 | The Family form | A child is a card. **Set…** opens a small window for the birth date and for the birthplace, and the button then shows what was typed. A long place fits. | [Add a family](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/add-a-family.dita) says so. |
| 7 | Search | Three buttons above the results show everything, only the people or only the events, as on the Android phone. Once the search has run, the box shows only the end of the word typed, such as "atrick". | The picture of the results is cut below the search box. |
| 8 | Sources | An empty list has **Add source** and **Compare records that disagree**, as on the tablet. Once the fact has a source, the second button is gone. Each source has a quality line, such as "Quality: 1 · Questionable". | [Look at the sources of a fact](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/look-at-the-sources-of-a-fact.dita) names both, and says when the second goes. |
| 9 | **Save as…** | The whole question shows, not cut off as on Android. After the save, a line at the bottom names the file and a folder, "File Provider Storage", which is not the name the save screen showed (On My iPhone). The line also counts the changes made to fit GEDCOM 7.0, and stays on the screen for a long time. | [Keep it safe](../../content/platforms/ios/topics/keep-it-safe.dita) does not name the folder from the line. |
| 10 | **Add from file…** | A box names the tree, counts what it holds, and says uFTi keeps its own copy. A card for a file whose name matches another card has the same name. | [Open a family tree file](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/open-a-family-tree-file.dita) tells the reader to use **Show details** to tell them apart. |
| 11 | A person's page | Charlotte's husband is listed under the heading **Parents**, with no heading of his own. | Not mentioned. The picture shows it. |
| 12 | **Photos** | Three buttons. When the page already has pictures, a fourth button shows, with an arrow that points down, which copies the pictures to a folder. In the list the chain button opens, a long description runs off the right edge. | [The Photos buttons](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/the-photos-buttons.dita) names all four. The list is not pictured: it shows the sample's own photographs. |
| 13 | The camera button | The iPhone asks if uFTi may use the camera. On the simulator, the app closed after **Allow**, because a simulator has no camera. | The page stops at the question. Taking a photo is for the next walk, on a real iPhone. |
| 14 | No person open | **Family tree** says "Select a person to view their family tree." **Person**, **Events** and **Notes** show an empty page under the search box, named Search. | [A page says no person is selected](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/a-page-says-no-person-selected.dita) covers both. |
| 15 | A person's form | **Edit** can open the form part of the way down, as on the tablet. | [Change a person](../../content/platforms/iphone/topics/change-a-person.dita) says to scroll up. |
| 16 | The Workspaces cards | A card with one family says "1 families", as on the tablet. | Not mentioned. |

### 3. What the tooling could not do

The pictures are copies of the whole simulator screen, with the status bar set to 10:00, Wi-Fi and a full battery, and then cut off. A box or a sheet is cut to itself. The iPhone's own Files screens, save screen, camera question and keyboard are never pictured, because they are the iPhone's, not the app's (DEC-184). The cutting and scaling are done before import, because the importer's own crop and resize run on Windows only. The iPhone's own boxes (New workspace, Remove workspace, Save as and the GEDCOM version) were answered by touch on the simulator; the rest of the app was driven by its test agent.
