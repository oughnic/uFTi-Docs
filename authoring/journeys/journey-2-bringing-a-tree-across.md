# Journey 2: Bringing a tree across

> "My tree is in another program or website."

Part of [WP0](README.md). Status: draft for testing, 16 October 2026. The format is from the [content model, section 4](../content-model.md#4-user-journeys). Screen names and labels are provisional until WP4 confirms them against the app.

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | They have kept a tree in another program or on a website for years. Now they want it on their phone, or a copy they hold themselves, or the old computer is being replaced. |
| **Person** | Switcher. A tree of hundreds or thousands of people, with photos and notes. They know the word "export". Some know the word GEDCOM. They have a Windows laptop and a phone. They worry: "Will I lose anything?" |
| **Devices** | Windows first, because the old program is on a computer. Then the phone. |
| **Success** | They searched for a grandparent and found them with their family. The number of people matches. They know what did not come across, and they can carry on. |
| **Session task** | "Here is a family tree file from another program. Get it into uFTi and find Charlotte Brontë." The file is an export of the Brontë sample. |

## Steps

| # | What they try, in their words | What they ask, and what would stop them | Content that answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Get my tree out of the old program." | "What file do I ask for?" The other program's menus are not ours to explain. | Task: Bring your tree from another program. It says which file to make and that photos may travel separately. Glossary: family tree file. |
| 2 | "Get the file onto this device." | "Where did it go on my phone?" "Can I email it to myself?" | Task (platform): Get a file onto your phone. Task (platform): Find a file on Windows. |
| 3 | "Open it in uFTi." | "Is it Import or Open?" "It asks me to name a workspace." "How long will it take?" | Task: Import a family tree file. Screen: a progress message with counts. |
| 4 | "Check everyone came across." | "It says 1,243 people. Is that right?" "How do I find Grandma?" | Task: Find a person. Reference: what the import summary shows. |
| 5 | "Find out what did not come across." | "Where are my photos?" "Some dates look different." "What are these warnings?" | Task: Check your tree. Reference: what an import keeps and what it does not. Guide: family tree files and their limits. |
| 6 | "Carry on where I left off." | "Is my old tree changed?" "Can I get it back out again?" | Task: Export a family tree file. Concept: workspaces. |

## On an Android phone

- Step 2 is the hard part. The file arrives by email or from a cloud drive. They open it from the Files app, or from uFTi's import, through the file picker.
- A big tree takes time to import. The phone must stay awake. To confirm.

## On Windows

- The file is usually in Downloads. Import opens a file dialog.
- A big tree is easier to check on a wide screen: the list, the page and the tree side by side. To confirm.

## On a tablet or an iPhone

- iPhone and iPad: the file arrives through the Files app or the share sheet. "Open in uFTi" from the share sheet is the natural route. To confirm.
- Tablet: the same as the phone, with more room to check the result.

## What this journey decides

- **Quick Start.** Its what-next link is "Bring your tree across". The Quick Start itself does not import: page 3 explores the sample instead.
- **Task finder.** "Bring in a tree from another program", "find someone in my tree", "check my tree for problems", "make a copy of my tree".
- **Help targets.** Import, the import summary, search, the check results.
- **Topics first.** Import a family tree file. Find a person. What an import keeps and what it does not.
- **For the app team (tier 0).** A summary after import: how many people, families, sources and photos came in, and what was skipped. A plain message when photos are missing.
