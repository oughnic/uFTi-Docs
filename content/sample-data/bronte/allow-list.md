# Allow-list for screenshots

The importer checks the focus person of every screenshot against this list ([screenshot strategy, section 5](../../../authoring/screenshot-strategy.md#5-import)). A screenshot whose focus is not on the list is rejected. The list is the people of the core specification, by the names the app will show. The [privacy check](privacy-check.md) is the evidence that every person on it died long ago.

## Not yet allowed

The extension people in [people.md](people.md#extensions-not-in-the-core-dec-046) (Elizabeth Branwell, Tabitha Aykroyd, Martha Brown, the two sets of parents) are not allowed until they are added to the core with their own evidence and privacy rows.

People from the app's other sample workspaces are allowed only when WP4 approves them by name from a named sample workspace, as the screenshot strategy provides.

## Two people share a name

P02 and P03 are both "Maria Brontë". A screen catalogue entry that uses either must say which, by id, and the capture script must open the right one. The manual itself uses this pair to show that the same name can belong to two people (stage 9, duplicates check).
