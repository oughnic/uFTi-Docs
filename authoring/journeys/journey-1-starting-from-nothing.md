# Journey 1: Starting from nothing

> "I know my grandparents' names."

Part of [WP0](README.md). Status: draft for testing, 16 October 2026. The format is from the [content model, section 4](../content-model.md#4-user-journeys). Screen names and labels are provisional until WP4 confirms them against the app.

| Field | |
|---|---|
| **Trigger** | A family event got them thinking: a funeral, a big birthday, or an old story told again over dinner. Someone said "you should write this down". They searched their app store for a family tree app, or a friend named uFTi. |
| **Person** | Newcomer. Nothing is written down. They know their parents and grandparents by name, and roughly where they lived. They have never done family history. They worry about getting it wrong. |
| **Devices** | Android phone, on the sofa in the evening. Or Windows at a desk, often at the weekend. |
| **Success** | They see three generations in the tree: themselves, their parents and their grandparents, joined up. They know how to come back and add more. |
| **Session task** | "Start a tree with you, your parents and one set of grandparents. You can use made-up names. Then show me the tree." |

## Steps

| # | What they try, in their words | What they ask, and what would stop them | Content that answers it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Get the app and open it." | "Is this the right one?" The first screen is quiet. It does not say what to do first. | Screen: the first-run screen. Quick Start page 1. |
| 2 | "Start a new tree." | "What is a workspace? Is that my tree?" "It asks who made this. Is that me?" The word *submitter* stops them. | Quick Start page 4. Task: Create a workspace. Glossary: workspace, submitter. |
| 3 | "Put myself in." | "Do I need my full name?" "Do I have to put my birthday?" | Quick Start page 4. Task: Add a person. |
| 4 | "Add my mum and dad." | "Do I add them as people first, or as a family?" "I added Mum but she is not joined to me." | Task: Add a parent, partner or child. Concept: People and families. |
| 5 | "Add my grandparents." | "What if I do not know Nan's maiden name?" "I only know Grandad died in the 1990s." | Task: Enter a date you are not sure of. Task: Enter a name you are not sure of. Reference: date formats. |
| 6 | "Look at it as a tree." | "Where is the tree?" "Why is Nan floating on her own?" She is not in a family yet. | Task: View the family tree. Concept: how families join people. |
| 7 | "Write down what Grandad told me." | "Where do I put a story?" | Task: Add a note. Guide: collect what the family already has. |
| 8 | "Make sure I will not lose it." | "Did it save?" "What if I lose my phone?" | Quick Start page 6. Task: Export a family tree file. |

## On an Android phone

- Page 1 is the phone's app store. The store name is to confirm.
- Typing is slow on a phone. Steps 3 to 5 take most of the ten minutes. Each form must be short.
- Step 8 is Export, then Share to an email or a cloud drive.

## On Windows

- Page 1 is the store or installer the Get uFTi page lists. To confirm.
- The screen is wide. The list and the page may show side by side, so step 4 is easier to follow. To confirm.
- Keyboard: Tab moves between controls and Enter chooses. Step 8 is Export, then choose a folder.

## On a tablet or an iPhone

- Tablet: the list and the page show side by side, like Windows, so "where did Mum go" is less of a problem. Pictures are landscape.
- iPhone: page 1 is the App Store. Step 8 shares through the share sheet. Everything else is the same as the phone.

## What this journey decides

- **Quick Start.** Pages 4 and 6 come from this journey. Its what-next link is "Build your first tree".
- **Task finder.** "Start my family tree", "add a person", "add someone's parents", "see my tree as a picture", "put in a date I am not sure of", "write down what someone told me", "make a copy of my tree".
- **Help targets.** Workspaces list, new workspace, add or edit a person, add or edit a family, the tree, add or edit a note, export.
- **Topics first.** Create a workspace. Add a person. Add a parent, partner or child. View the family tree. Enter a date you are not sure of. Add a note. Export a family tree file.
- **For the app team (tier 0).** The first screen needs one line that says what to do. The word *submitter* needs a plain label or a hint. A person who is in no family needs a nudge: "Add this person to a family".
