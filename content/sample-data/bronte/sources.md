# Sources

Everything we consulted, numbered so that the other files and the [evidence register](evidence-register.md) can cite it. Stage 6 adds a selection of these to the workspace as sources held in [repositories](repositories.md); the rest are the working references behind the register.

Columns:

- **Kind** follows the good practice guide: an *original* record, a *derivative* (a transcription, index, abstract or catalogue made from an original), or an *authored* work (a biography, article or web page).
- **How consulted** says what we actually looked at. If we read a transcription or an index, we say so and do not claim to have seen the original.
- **Reference** is copied from the item, never reconstructed.
- **Consulted** is the date we opened it. All were opened on 16 October 2026.
