Say who made this tree

Add your name, or the name of a group, as the maker of your tree.

About this task

The page Who made this tree lists the people or groups credited with the tree. It is usually only you. The name marked (default) is named as the maker when you save the tree.

Procedure

  1. Choose Who made this tree in the menu.

    If nobody is credited yet, the page says Nobody is credited with this tree yet. Use the + button to add a name.

  2. Choose the + button, named Add a record, at the top of the window.

    A sheet named Add a record opens.

  3. In the group Add to the workspace, choose Submitter, then choose Add.

    The group is near the end of the sheet. Scroll to it if you cannot see it. Add does not work until you choose a form.

    A form named Submitter opens.

  4. Type your name in Name.

    This is the only box you must fill in. The form also has Address, Telephone, email and website, Language and Copyright of this tree. For a telephone number, an email address or a website, choose Add row.

  5. Choose Save.

    To leave without saving, choose Cancel.

Results

The page now lists your name, with (default) after it. Beside it are a button for notes, and the buttons Edit and Delete.

Until you fill in Copyright of this tree, the page says This tree states no copyright. To add one later, choose Edit beside a name and fill in that box.

The picture shows the page with one name listed, uFTi documentation (default), and that line about copyright.
