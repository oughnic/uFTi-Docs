# Privacy check

The rule: no living person appears in the dataset or in a screenshot ([dataset strategy, section 9](../../../authoring/dataset-strategy.md#9-privacy-and-care)). This check confirms, from sources, that every person in the core died long ago and that none of Patrick's six children is known to have left children. It was drafted on 16 October 2026. The second reader (Q20) has not yet countersigned it.

## None of the six children is known to have left children

| Id | Person | What the sources say | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| P03 | Maria | Died aged about 11 | S03, S18 |
| P04 | Elizabeth | Died aged about 10 | S03, S18 |
| P05 | Charlotte | Married in June 1854 and died in March 1855. The tablet names her only as wife and daughter. Her will, made six weeks before her death, provides for a child "in case I leave issue"; the probate act names her husband as sole executor and no child. Her husband's later life is described with no child of this marriage | S03, S08, S07, S24 |
| P06 | Branwell | Unmarried in every account; the tablet names him as son only | S02, S03, S06 |
| P07 | Emily | Unmarried in every account; the tablet names her as daughter only | S02, S03, S06 |
| P08 | Anne | Unmarried in every account; the tablet names her as daughter only | S02, S03, S06 |

Gaskell calls Patrick, after Charlotte's death, "the childless and widowed father" and Charlotte "the last of that nursery of six" (S01). The DNB says the father "survived her" (S06). A 2024 account of Arthur Bell Nicholls's second marriage says "They had no children" (S24).

**Conclusion.** No descendant of Patrick and Maria Brontë is recorded in any source we opened, and the sources that describe each child's life record none. Arthur Bell Nicholls had no children by either marriage according to the one source that addresses it. We therefore find no living person who could appear in the core dataset. Confidence: high for the six children; moderate for Nicholls's second marriage, which rests on one source (a second reader should check it against the Irish civil records, R09).

## Other people named in the sources

Gaskell, Shorter and the DNB name many other people: schoolfellows, employers, publishers, servants, clergy. None of them is in the dataset, and none may appear on a screen. A capture script that shows a search result list must show only people from the specification.

## What this check does not do

It does not claim to be a complete genealogy. It checks the nine people we use. If the core changes, this file changes in the same pull request.
