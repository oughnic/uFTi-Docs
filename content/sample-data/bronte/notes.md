# Notes

The notes the dataset holds. Stage 5 adds them: one shared note, short notes on people and families, and one research note per thread. All text is in our own words. Where a note quotes a source, the quotation is short and the source is named.

Research notes carry the question and the conclusion. The full working of each thread is in [research-threads.md](research-threads.md); in the app, the research note holds the same text.

## Research notes (one per thread)

| Id | Attached to | Question | Conclusion recorded in the note | Thread |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| N11 | P01 | What was Patrick's surname at birth, and when did it become Brontë? | The Irish register spells it Brunty; Prunty and Branty also occur; he signed Brontë from his curacies onward. Which spelling the family used is open. Confidence in the change itself: high. | T01 |
| N12 | P01 | Where in County Down was Patrick born? | Two sources name the townland Emdale; two name the parish Ahaderg; two say only County Down. We record Emdale with moderate confidence and leave the parish open. | T02 |
| N13 | P06, P07, P08 | Why do the memorials give different ages for Branwell, Emily and Anne? | The first tablet understates each age by one year, and Anne's gravestone by one. The baptism dates support the replacement tablet. Concluded: 31, 30 and 29. Confidence: high. | T03 |
| N14 | P03, P04, P08 | In which year were Maria, Elizabeth and Anne born? | Published works differ by a year for each. The baptisms and the memorial ages bound the years but do not settle them. Open until the registers are read. | T04 |
| N15 | P01, X01 | Why does the 1841 census give Patrick's age as 60? | The 1841 census rounded adults' ages down to a multiple of five, so 60 is what the rules required for a man of 64. Open until the image is read: our only reading of the return is second-hand. | T05 |
