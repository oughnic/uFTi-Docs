# The Alpha-1 Android phone walk

What was walked, what was found and what the manual does about it. Part of the [authoring plan](../authoring-plan.md). Written 27 October 2026 for [issue 103](https://github.com/oughnic/uFTi-Docs/issues/103). Decisions: DEC-171 and DEC-172. The Windows walk is [alpha-1-windows.md](alpha-1-windows.md).

The app is the Alpha-1 build for Android (shown in the app as version 0.1.0), in the Oxford and slate theme and the light look, on the reference phone (a Samsung Galaxy A41, Android 12, portrait, font size 1.0). The data is the Brontë dataset only. The pictures are made by the same journeys as the Windows walk. This report says what a reader can see. It names no code, no issue and no build of the application repository (DEC-040, and the standing rule that this repository is public).

## 1. What was walked

- **First run.** The Workspaces screen, with the app's own samples. The Brontë sample is one of them, and it is the tree the pictures of Charlotte, the events and the sources come from.
- **Create a workspace.** The New workspace box, then the New person form straight away. Patrick Brontë was added with dates and places. Maria Branwell was added from the **+** sheet, by **Basic details**.
- **Add a family.** Patrick and Maria, their marriage and Charlotte as a new child, with the small windows for her birth date and place. The family page and its tree.
- **A source and a note.** One source on Patrick's birth, through the whole run of screens. One note on Patrick.
- **A copy.** **Save as…** and the two choices, as far as Android's own save screen, which was left without saving.
- **Every place in the bar and in the More list.** Search and its **Refine by** list, a person's page, an event, the sources of a fact, the **+** sheet in full, the three empty lists, **Preferences**.
- **Remove a tree from the list**, as far as the question, which was cancelled.

Not walked: the camera, the folder and the file buttons under **Photos**, **Add from file…** (Android's own file screen lists other things on the phone), the Android save screen, a GEDZIP copy, the forms for a census or a register entry, rotation, and the **About uFTi** page. The manual does not teach them yet.

| # | Where | What a reader sees | What the manual does |

| 3 | New person, Family and Note forms | A grey button named **Fill from document…** shows. Documents are out of scope. | Cropped out of the pictures and not described. |
| 4 | Saving a new person | The app goes back to the screen it came from. No message was seen. | The topic says that. |
| 5 | **Save as…** | The question is cut off in the middle of a line (**Everything keeps the tree, notes and pictures; the fami…**). Then comes Android's own save screen, which lists the other files on the phone. | [Keep it safe](../../content/platforms/android/topics/keep-it-safe.dita) names the choices and never pictures the save screen. |
| 6 | A page opened from **More** | The notes page opened from More shows the top bar without its search box, and the **Showing** chip over the heading. | The picture of notes is one taken from a clean screen. |
| 7 | The top of every screen | The name of the tree shows twice at the left, as the first two words of the bar. | Pictures are not cropped for it. |
| 8 | **More** | **Notes**, **Where records are kept**, **Who made this tree**, **Research questions and notes**, **Preferences** and **About uFTi**. The first three are empty until something is added. | [The screens of the app](../../content/platforms/android-phone/topics/tour-of-the-screens.dita). |
| 9 | The family tree | The boxes are small. **Fit to screen** shows the whole drawing, but a big family looks very small. | The topic says to choose **Fit to screen**. |
| 10 | The search box | The words typed stay in the box when you go to another place. | Not mentioned. |
| 11 | **Photos** | The list that the chain button opens shows the pictures of the Brontë sample. | Not pictured: the sample's own pictures are not ours to show. |

## 3. What the tooling could not do

The agent shows the screen of the app only, without sheets and native boxes. Those pictures are cut from a copy of the whole screen of the phone, with the bar at the top and the bar at the bottom taken off (rows 90 to 2272). The phone's own save screen, file screen and keyboard are never pictured, because they show the other things on the phone. A box with the keyboard open is cropped to the box (DEC-172).
